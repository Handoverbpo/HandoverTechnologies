03 — The checkpoint pipeline

One QR scan. Six checkpoints. Zero guesswork.

Every vehicle moves through the same six gates from entry to dispatch. A few seconds with a phone camera at each one writes the depot's story as it happens, not from memory at the end of the week.

  1. Entry

    Logged the moment it crosses the gate: registration, driver, time.

  2. Fuel

    Fuel level captured before the vehicle moves further into the yard.

  3. Depot intake

    Odometer, condition notes and damage flagged on arrival, not disputed later.

  4. Cleaning

    Wash and valet status tracked so nothing dispatches half-done.

  5. Dispatch ready

    Signed off as fit to go, the last check before the road.

  6. Dispatch out

    The exit scan closes the loop and resets the dwell-time clock.

04 — What you get

Everything your ops manager currently keeps in their head.

Every vehicle, live

One board shows every vehicle on site and exactly which checkpoint it's at, right now. No more phone calls to the yard.

Dwell time, automatically

The clock starts at entry and splits by checkpoint, so you can see exactly where a vehicle lost time, and who was responsible.

Alerts before it's a problem

Vehicles that overstay a threshold, miss a checkpoint or come due for a service get flagged before a client or auditor notices.

A record for every scan

Who scanned it, where and when, timestamped and unchangeable. When there's a dispute, you have the answer, not an opinion.

One system, every branch

Run one depot or thirteen. Every branch reports into the same fleet register, with consistent checkpoints across all of them.

The right screen per role

Gate staff get a scan screen. Managers get the dashboard. Nobody sees (or accidentally edits) more than their role needs.
