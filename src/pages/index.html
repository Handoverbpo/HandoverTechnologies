03 — How we work

From first call to handover.

A clear, fixed-scope process. You always know what's being built, what it costs and when it lands.

  1. Discover

    A free consult to map your goals, systems and bottlenecks. You get a written plan and a fixed quote.

  2. Design

    We prototype the flows, dashboards or pages so you can see and approve them before we build.

  3. Build

    Short sprints with weekly demos. Tested against your real data and real customers.

  4. Hand over

    Training and documentation, then a monthly retainer for monitoring, updates and a dedicated consultant.
