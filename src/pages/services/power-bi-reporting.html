FAQ

Power BI questions.

From R13 000 setup, then R3 200 / month. See full pricing.

Do we need Power BI Pro licences?

To share reports in the Power BI service, each viewer typically needs a Pro (or Premium Per User) licence, unless you use Fabric capacity. Microsoft licensing is billed separately. We'll advise the most cost-effective setup.

Our data is messy. Can you still help?

Yes, that's most projects. We clean and transform data as part of the model, and highlight data-quality issues to fix at the source.

Can you take over existing reports?

Absolutely. We audit and optimise existing Power BI files, fix slow refreshes and rebuild models so they scale.

Can our clients see their own reports?

Yes. With row-level security and Power BI Embedded (or our Handover Lens portal), each client sees only their own data.
