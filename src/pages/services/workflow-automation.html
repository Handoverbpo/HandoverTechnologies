FAQ

Automation questions.

Full System from R16 500 setup, then R3 200 / month. See full pricing.

Which systems can you connect?

Most modern cloud tools, including Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Sage, Xero, QuickBooks, HubSpot, Salesforce, Shopify and WooCommerce, plus databases and anything with an API. For older systems we can work with exports, email or custom connectors.

Do I need extra software licences?

Sometimes. Power Automate premium connectors or a Make/n8n plan may be required. We'll recommend the most cost-effective option and include it in your quote. Licences are billed in your name so you own them.

What happens if an automation breaks?

Every flow includes error handling and alerts. On a support retainer we monitor and fix issues proactively; otherwise you have runbooks to troubleshoot, or you can log a request with us at any time.

How quickly will I see a return?

Most systems are live within 14 days and pay for themselves within a few months in time saved. We estimate this for you during the audit.
