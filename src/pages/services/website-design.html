Website questions.

How long does a new website take?

A typical five- to ten-page business website takes four to six weeks from approved content. Larger sites or e-commerce take longer; we confirm the timeline in your quote.

Do I own the website?

Once paid in full, you own the custom deliverables we create for you, and your domain and hosting accounts stay in your name. Our reusable templates and frameworks remain ours, as set out in our Terms.

Can you take over my existing website?

Yes. We'll audit it first, then either move it onto a care plan or recommend a rebuild if that's more cost-effective.

Do you write the content?

We can. Copywriting and SEO content are available as add-ons, or we'll guide you with a simple content template.
