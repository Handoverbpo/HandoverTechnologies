FAQ

Chatbot questions.

From R9 000 setup, then R2 200 / month. See full pricing.

Do I need a separate WhatsApp number?

Yes. The WhatsApp Business Platform needs a number that isn't active on the regular WhatsApp app. We can migrate an existing number or help you get a new one, and we handle the Meta verification process with you.

How many conversations are included?

Starter includes up to 300 conversations a month and Full System up to 800. Need more? We'll quote a custom volume. Any Meta / WhatsApp platform subscriptions remain in your name, as set out in our Terms.

Will the bot make things up?

No. We ground the assistant in your approved content and set strict rules. If it doesn't know, it says so and hands over to a person.

Is it POPIA compliant?

We design every bot with consent capture, opt-out handling, data minimisation and secure storage, and help you update your privacy notice. You remain the responsible party for your customers' data.

Can it speak Afrikaans, isiZulu or other languages?

Yes. The assistant can detect and reply in many South African languages. We test each language you enable before launch.
