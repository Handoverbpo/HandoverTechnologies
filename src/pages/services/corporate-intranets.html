How long does an intranet project take?

A focused SharePoint intranet typically takes six to ten weeks. Larger projects with migrations and custom workflows take longer; we'll give you a fixed timeline after discovery.

Can you move our documents from file servers?

Yes. We plan and run migrations from network drives, Google Drive, Dropbox and older SharePoint versions, cleaning up permissions along the way.

Do staff need extra licences?

For a SharePoint intranet, most Microsoft 365 business and enterprise plans already include what you need. Frontline staff may need F-plans; we'll check your licensing.
