Pricing FAQ

The fine print, made plain.

The full details are in our Terms & Conditions.

Do your prices include VAT?

Yes. All prices are in South African rand (ZAR) and include VAT.

How do payments work?

A 50% deposit of the setup fee is payable before work starts, and the balance on delivery or go-live. Monthly retainers are billed in advance and can be paid by DebiCheck, EFT or card.

Is there a minimum term?

Yes. Recurring services have a three-month minimum term. After that, either party can cancel with 30 days' written notice.

What does the monthly retainer cover?

Monitoring, updates and support for the system we've built, with a dedicated consultant, so it keeps running long after launch.

Do you offer bundle discounts?

Yes. Bundle two or more services and save 10–15%. Custom scopes and multi-service bundles are quoted on request.

How quickly can we go live?

Most systems are live within 14 days. We handle design, configuration, testing and deployment, and you review and approve before launch.
