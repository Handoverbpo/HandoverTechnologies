About Handover

We build it. We run it. Then we hand it over.

Handover Technologies is a South African software studio. We help businesses replace manual work, scattered data and outdated tools with systems that quietly do their job, and we make sure our clients own and understand every one of them.

“The best technology is the kind you stop noticing because it simply works.”

Our story

Why we're called Handover.

Too many businesses are held hostage by their own technology: a website only one freelancer can edit, reports that live in someone's personal spreadsheet, automations nobody understands.

We started Handover Technologies to do it differently. Every system we deliver comes with clear documentation and training, your accounts and data stay in your name, and the custom work we deliver is yours once paid. A proper handover is the finish line of every project, not an afterthought.

Every system ships with a maintenance plan, so nothing we build is left to rot. Our retainers keep it monitored, updated and improving, with a dedicated consultant who knows your setup.

What we believe

Four principles, every project.

Systems over headcount

Instead of adding people to a problem, we build the infrastructure that removes it, and it scales without the overhead.

Demo-first

You see a working version before you commit, not a pitch deck.

Plain language

We explain what we're building and why, without jargon. If you can't understand it, we haven't explained it well enough.

Outcomes over output

We measure success in hours saved, leads captured and decisions made faster, not in features shipped.

Privacy first

POPIA isn't a checkbox. Consent, security and data minimisation are designed into everything we build.
