3. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Cookies help websites remember user preferences, improve website performance, maintain security, analyse website usage, enable functionality, and support user authentication. Cookies do not typically contain information that directly identifies an individual, although information associated with cookies may be linked to other information held by a website operator.

4. Similar Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use web beacons, pixels, local storage, session storage, analytics tags, tracking scripts, log files, and device identifiers. These technologies perform functions similar to cookies and may collect information about interactions with our services.

5. Types of Cookies We Use

5.1 Essential Cookies — These cookies are necessary for website operation and security, including login authentication, session management, security verification, fraud prevention, and system administration. Without these cookies, certain website features may not function correctly. These cookies cannot generally be disabled through our systems.

5.2 Functional Cookies — These cookies help remember language preferences, user settings, form information, accessibility preferences, and website customisation choices. These cookies improve the user experience but are not strictly required for website functionality.

5.3 Analytics and Performance Cookies — These cookies help us understand how visitors use our website, which pages are viewed, user navigation behaviour, performance issues, and traffic patterns. Information collected is generally aggregated and used to improve our services. Examples may include Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, and similar website analytics platforms.

5.4 Marketing and Advertising Cookies — Where applicable, these cookies may be used to measure marketing effectiveness, understand campaign performance, improve advertising relevance, and track conversions. These cookies may be placed by Handover or approved third-party advertising providers.

5.5 Security Cookies — Security cookies assist with account protection, login verification, bot detection, abuse prevention, fraud monitoring, and platform integrity. These cookies help protect both users and systems.

6. DepotTrack and Client Systems

9. Cross-Border Data Processing

10. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow users to view, delete, block, limit, and manage tracking preferences for cookies. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality, user experience, account access, and system performance.

11. Cookie Consent

Where required by applicable law, users may be presented with a cookie banner or consent mechanism when visiting our website. Users may accept cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or modify cookie preferences. Continued use of our website may be interpreted as consent to the use of cookies where legally permissible.
